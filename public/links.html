Remember links pages? Before algorithms picked what you saw next, people just pointed at sites they liked. This is that. Click around, get lost, find something good.

Got a feed reader? Grab the blogroll as an OPML file and import every site below that has a feed in one go.

Gardens I Wander Through

  • Andy Matuschak
    Research on tools for thought, and the evergreen notes that half the gardens on this list grew out of.
  • Maggie Appleton feed
    Illustrated essays and notes on programming, design, and anthropology. The garden everyone points to, for good reason.
  • Mental Nodes
    A public notebook built on the idea that the only way to learn in public is to build in public.
  • Mosaic Muse
    Soren Bjornstad's public notes: a deep, tidy web of linked ideas.
  • Yenly Ma feed
    Inspirations, ideas, and reflections on learning and making. Wabi-sabi to the core.
  • Dave's Notes and Rambles
    Incomplete thoughts left to compost in public. Proof a garden never has to be finished.
  • Joseph Jude's Notes
    A CTO and coach's commonplace book, from someone who has kept a home on the web since 2003.
  • mistermicheels' Learning Notes
    Learning notes, written down while the learning is still happening.
  • mister chad's Mind Garden
    A mind garden of linked notes to wander through without a map.
  • Joel Hooks
    A digital garden about teaching, technology, and the web.
  • Bryan Jenks
    A zettelkasten-style garden of interlinked notes.
  • Joel Chan's Research Garden
    Research questions and claims about analogy and creativity, laid out as a garden.
  • Chris Aldrich (BoffoSocko) feed
    A digital commonplace book on the IndieWeb, note taking, history, and a little bit of everything.
  • EDAV Garden
    Course notes on exploratory data analysis, grown with the same Obsidian plugin as my own garden.
  • Aaron Young
    A trail runner and JavaScript developer's curated notes on everything he does.
  • Notathagen
    A Norwegian garden written in Obsidian, typos and messy structure proudly included.
  • manim's Notes
    Coding, physics, and whatever else got picked up along the way.
  • John's Digital Galaxy
    A garden of notes and maps of content, drawn up as a galaxy.
  • Sébastien Dubois feed
    Personal organization, Obsidian, and knowledge work, from someone who just left Obsidian Publish to build his own notes site.
  • Joshua Rodrigues feed
    A small garden whose "Why a garden" page makes the case better than most.
  • CraftEngineer
    Software development and engineering management, from someone crafting software since 1994.
  • Fabien Benetou
    A personal information manager published as a wiki, with the whole thing out in the open.

Required Reading for Garden Nerds

  • The Digital Gardeners list
    Maggie Appleton's big list of gardens, tools, and essays. Start here and lose a weekend.
  • The Garden and the Stream feed
    Mike Caulfield's 2015 talk that set gardens against streams. Where the whole idea starts.
  • Digital Garden Terms of Service feed
    swyx's ground rules for reading half-finished notes, and for writing them.
  • How to Build a Digital Garden with TiddlyWiki feed
    Ness Labs' step-by-step guide to an older, weirder way to garden.
  • Derek Sivers
    Short, sharp essays from the man who gave us the now page.
  • nownownow.com
    A directory of personal sites with a now page, like mine.
