Fluharty Alley

In a world that seems to be stuck in fast-forward, I simply want a space to do work that means something and fills my bucket. Join me on my journey to Learn in Public, cultivate a Digitial Garden, and find meaningful, collaborative, connections.

Door no. 1 DIGITAL GARDEN The Bearly Tended Garden is where you will find everything of substance (and lots of extras). Fleeting thoughts, Personal Knowledge Slipbox, opinions nobody asked for, Rants, Musings, half-formed ideas and long-form essays and blog style notes. Rarely is a finished product going to be found as these notes are always growing and evolving with new information and experiences gained by yours truly. Step through the gate → OPEN
Door no. 2 NOW PAGE You see an old friend at the bar, home from college for the holidays. Excitedly, you give them an embrace and ask what they have been up to. You don't care about what they had for dinner last night or the woman that cut you off in traffic, you want the stories of substance that mark their journey through life. Okay, and *maybe* you want to know what they thought of the latest Boys III Men album. This regularly updated page will give you the rundown on what I am doing nowadays and some of the little things that remind me why life is worth living. Take a Peek → LIVE
