Colophon

the structure, presentation, and technical details

The Build

HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. The way the old gods intended.

Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with Cloudflare running the DNS and such. Claude Code helped me assemble the code.

The Aesthetic

90s nostalgia: neon bar signs, wheatpaste posters, spray paint, and Hypercolor. Think of a blacktop basketball court, remember the carpet floor in the arcade room of the skating rink, hear the sound of a ghetto blaster somewhere in the distance.

  • Alley Concrete
  • Wheatpaste
  • Radioactive Lime
  • Electric Pink
  • Cyan Burner
  • Traffic Cone
  • Grape Soda

The Fonts

Tilt Neon and Neonderthaw for the neon tubes, Rubik Dirt for the posters, Permanent Marker for the tags, and Work Sans for everything you actually have to read. All served straight from this site.

The Principles

Fun shouldn't cost anyone access. Every animation on this site respects prefers-reduced-motion, every page has a skip link, and the markup is semantic HTML underneath all the spray paint.

Everything Else

The living, growing stuff — notes, essays, half-formed ideas — lives in the digital garden, powered by Obsidian. This site is just the front door.
