The Bones

HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. The way the old gods intended.

Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with Cloudflare running the DNS and backend operations. Claude Code helped me assemble the code.

The Visuals

As much 90s nostalgia from my childhood that I could fit onto the site: neon bar signs, wheatpaste posters, spray paint, and Hypercolor. Think of a blacktop basketball court, remember the carpet floor in the arcade room of the skating rink, hear the sound of a ghetto blaster somewhere in the distance.

The sign over the entrance reads Fluharty Alley, and the lime neon bear in your browser tab is the house mascot.

The Fonts

Tilt Neon and Neonderthaw for the neon tubes, Rubik Dirt for the posters, Permanent Marker for the tags, and Work Sans for everything you actually have to read. All served straight from this site.

Accessibility

Fun shouldn't cost anyone access. Every animation on this site respects prefers-reduced-motion, every page has a skip link, and the markup is semantic HTML underneath all the spray paint.

Everything Else

The living, growing stuff — notes, essays, half-formed ideas — lives in the digital garden, powered by Obsidian. the Now Page, from the mind of Derek Sivers keeps you updated on the big stuff going on, as well as little bits I think are interesting to know. This site is just the front door.
