The Bones

HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, written by hand. No frameworks, no build step, no npm install. The way the old gods intended.

The one bit of server code is a tiny Cloudflare Pages Function that asks Cloudflare how many visits the alley got in the last 30 days, so the counter on the homepage shows a real number.

The source lives on GitHub, if you want to see how the sausage gets made.

Hosting

Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which also runs the DNS. Merging a change on GitHub publishes it automatically, so there's no FTP and no upload button. Email runs through Proton.

Help From a Robot

Claude Code helps me write and maintain the code. The ideas, the opinions, and the questionable taste in 90s nostalgia are all mine.

The Visuals

As much 90s nostalgia from my childhood that I could fit onto the site: neon bar signs, wheatpaste posters, spray paint, and Hypercolor. Think of a blacktop basketball court, remember the carpet floor in the arcade room of the skating rink, hear the sound of a ghetto blaster somewhere in the distance.

The sign over the entrance reads Fluharty Alley, and the lime neon bear in your browser tab is the house mascot, drawn for this site.

The Fonts

Tilt Neon and Neonderthaw for the neon tubes, Rubik Dirt for the posters, Permanent Marker for the tags, and Work Sans for everything you actually have to read.

All of them are served straight from this site, so loading them doesn't ping anyone else. Permanent Marker is under the Apache License 2.0; the rest are under the SIL Open Font License.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking cookies, nothing following you around the internet. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies. The whole story is in the privacy policy.

Accessibility

Fun shouldn't cost anyone access. Every animation on this site respects prefers-reduced-motion, everything works from a keyboard, and the markup is semantic HTML underneath all the spray paint.

Every page also has a "Skip to content" link for keyboard and screen reader users. It stays hidden until you need it: press Tab when a page first loads and it pops up in the top corner.

Weight

Every page, fonts and all, comes in under 400 KB, and about half of that is the grit baked into Rubik Dirt. No video, no trackers, no megabytes of JavaScript.

IndieWeb Bits

The homepage carries an h-card and rel="me" links, so my profiles elsewhere can vouch that this domain is really me. The now page is an idea borrowed from Derek Sivers, and this page is a colophon.

The living, growing stuff (notes, essays, half-formed ideas) lives in the digital garden, powered by Obsidian. This site is just the front door.
