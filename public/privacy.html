Privacy

last updated 2026/11/23

The Short Version

This is a one-person linktree and presentation platform with a side order of data/knowledge storage. I don't have accounts for you to create, forms to fill out, or anything to sell you. The only thing that watches you here is a cookie-free visitor count. There are no ad networks and no trackers hawking your data to third parties.

What Information Gets Collected

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see roughly how many people visit and which pages they land on. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and, according to Cloudflare, doesn't fingerprint you; it counts page views, not people. What it records: the page you visited, the site that sent you here, your approximate country, your device and browser type, and how fast the page loaded.

The Hit Counter

The retro odometer on the homepage counts your visits using a number stored in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device and I can't see it, it exists simply to be charming and be a relic if a time long past.

Fonts

The typefaces are served from this site itself, so loading them doesn't involve anyone else.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees your IP address and basic request details when a page loads.

Everything Else

No forms, no newsletter, no accounts, and no cookies at all. If you email me, that's a regular email; Proton (my secure mail client) handles it same as any other.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Not for analytics, not for anything. Block them all if you like; the site works exactly the same either way.

Other Sites

Links here point out to places like the digital garden, Goodreads, and various social platforms. Once you click through, you're on their turf and covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

Questions
