Privacy

last updated 2026/11/23

The Short Version

This is one person's corner of the internet, not a business. There are no ads, no accounts, no forms, and nothing for sale. I don't sell or share anything about you, I don't build profiles, and nothing here tracks you from site to site. The only thing keeping count is a cookie-free visitor tally from Cloudflare.

This policy covers the whole alley: fluharty.link, the digital garden at garden.fluharty.link, and any other page that lives under fluharty.link.

Who's Responsible

Me, Jacob Fluharty. I run the alley on my own, for personal and non-commercial purposes. Reach me at jacob@fluharty.link about anything in this policy.

What Information Gets Collected

Hosting and Security

Every page is delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare sees your IP address, your browser and device type, the page you asked for, and when. It uses that to deliver pages and to block attacks and abusive bots. If Cloudflare needs to check that you're a person, it may set one security cookie (cf_clearance) that does nothing else. Cloudflare also scrambles email addresses on these pages so scraper bots can't harvest them. I don't keep server logs of my own.

Visitor Counts

Both the main site and the garden use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see roughly how many people visit and which pages they land on. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and, according to Cloudflare, doesn't fingerprint you; it counts page views, not people. What it records: the page you visited, the site that sent you here, your approximate country, your device and browser type, and how fast the page loaded.

The hit counter in the homepage footer shows the total from those same counts: visits to the alley in the last 30 days, as one number. Showing it collects nothing new and stores nothing on your device.

Things Kept in Your Own Browser

The garden saves one small note in your browser's localStorage: which "map of content" sections you opened or closed, so they stay that way when you come back (keys starting with moc-). It never leaves your device and I can't see it. Clearing your browser's site data for garden.fluharty.link removes it.

The hit counter used to keep a per-browser count the same way. It doesn't anymore, and the main site now deletes that old count (flu-visits) from your browser if it finds one.

Code Libraries in the Garden

To draw the note graph, power search, render diagrams, and show icons, the garden asks your browser to load open-source code from public libraries: jsDelivr, cdnjs, and unpkg. Like any server your browser downloads from, they see your IP address and browser type when that code loads. They set no cookies.

Fonts

The main site's typefaces are served from fluharty.link itself, so loading them doesn't involve anyone else.

Email

If you email me, I get what you send: your address and your message. My mail runs through Proton. I keep your emails as long as the conversation is useful, and I'll delete them if you ask.

Why, and on What Basis

Everything above exists to deliver pages, keep the alley safe from abuse, and get a rough sense of what people read. Under European data protection law, that's my legitimate interest in running a small website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); the email you send me is handled to reply to you. No advertising, no profiling, no automated decisions about you, and nothing is sold or shared in the sense of California's privacy law.

Cookies and Browser Storage

No tracking or advertising cookies, anywhere in the alley. The only cookie you might get is Cloudflare's security cookie described above, which is strictly necessary to keep the sites working and safe. The browser notes listed above are the only other things stored on your device. Block or clear any of it whenever you like.

Who Else Handles Data

  • Cloudflare hosts both sites, protects them, and runs the visitor counts. It's a US company that participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and falls back on the EU's standard contractual clauses for transfers out of Europe.
  • jsDelivr, cdnjs, and unpkg serve the garden's code libraries.
  • Proton, based in Switzerland, handles my email.

How Long It's Kept

I don't keep any visitor data myself. Cloudflare keeps request and analytics data for a limited time under its own privacy policy. The notes in your browser stay until you clear them.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. In practice I hold nothing about you unless you've emailed me, but ask anyway and I'll answer. If you're in the EU, the UK, or anywhere with similar laws, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are welcome here, though there's nothing to opt out of: no tracking, no selling, no sharing.

Kids

Nothing here is aimed at children, and I don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 16.

Other Sites

Links here point out to places like Goodreads, YouTube, and various social platforms. Once you click through, you're on their turf and covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

Questions
